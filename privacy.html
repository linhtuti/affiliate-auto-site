Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Affiliate Content Assistant (“Service”) handles information when a creator or affiliate publisher uses the Service with an authorized social-media account.

1. Information we handle

Depending on the features used, we may handle product data, draft content, media URLs, publishing status, platform account identifiers, and technical logs needed to operate workflows. We do not request or store your social-media password.

2. Connected services

When you authorize a platform integration, the relevant platform may provide access tokens or account identifiers to enable the requested action. We use those credentials only for the authorized workflow and store them in the configured automation system. Review the privacy policies of the connected platforms for their own processing.

3. How information is used

Information is used to generate and review content, publish content that you approve, prevent duplicate actions, record errors, and measure workflow performance. We do not sell personal information.

4. Retention and security

Information is retained only as long as needed for the workflow, audit trail, and legal or operational requirements. Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

5. Your choices

You may revoke a platform authorization, request deletion of information held by the Service, or ask questions by contacting us. Some records may need to be retained where required for security, dispute handling, or legal compliance.
